# Research Notes

## 1. How does the directive define statutory interest for late payment?

**Answer:** Statutory interest for late payment is simple interest at a rate equal to the reference rate plus at least eight percentage points.

**Citation:** Source 1, Article 2(6).

## 2. The owner's note says the EU maximum late fee is 25 EUR. Is that right?

**Answer:** The sources do not support the owner's claim that the EU maximum late fee is EUR 25. Source 1, Article 6(1), states that the creditor is entitled to a minimum fixed sum of EUR 40 when interest for late payment becomes payable under Article 3 or Article 4. Article 6(3) also provides for reasonable compensation for recovery costs exceeding that fixed sum. Therefore, the directive specifies a minimum fixed compensation amount, not a maximum of EUR 25.

**Citation:** Source 1, Article 6(1)–(3).

## 3. Does the business have to send a reminder before it is entitled to interest and to the fixed compensation sum?

**Answer:** No. For commercial transactions between undertakings, Article 3(1) states that the creditor is entitled to interest for late payment without the necessity of a reminder, provided the creditor has fulfilled its contractual and legal obligations and has not received the amount due on time, unless the debtor is not responsible for the delay. Article 6(2) states that the fixed compensation sum is also payable without the necessity of a reminder, where interest for late payment becomes payable under Article 3 or Article 4.

**Citation:** Source 1, Articles 3(1) and 6(1)–(2).

## 4. What is the current ECB reference rate?

**Answer:** Not in the sources. Source 1, Article 2(7)(a), describes the reference rate for a Member State whose currency is the euro by reference to certain European Central Bank interest rates. However, the supplied extract does not give the current numerical rate. The current applicable rate would need to be checked using an up-to-date authoritative ECB source, which is outside the permitted source documents for this research task.

**Citation:** Source 1, Article 2(7)(a) and Article 3(2).
